C. Clean: Code

watches <- watches |>
  unnest(subtitles, keep_empty = TRUE) |>
  rename(channel = name) |>
  mutate(
    title    = str_replace(title, "^Watched ", ""),   # anchor: only at the start
    video_id = str_extract(titleUrl, "[^=]+$"),        # everything after the last "="
    artist   = str_replace(title, " - .*", ""),        # drop " - " and everything after
    song     = str_replace(title, "^[^-]+ - ", "")     # drop everything up to the first " - "
  )

searches <- searches |>
  mutate(terms = str_extract(titleUrl, "[^=]+$"))

watches  |> select(title, channel, video_id, artist, song) |> gt()
searches |> select(title, terms) |> gt()

span_days <- round(log_span / ddays(1))

events |>

D. Was I in Class?: Code

schedule <- tibble(
  day = c("Mon", "Wed", "Fri", "Mon", "Mon", "Wed", "Fri", "Tue", "Tue", "Thu", "Tue"),
  course = c("Stat 133", "Stat 133", "Stat 133", "Stat 133",
             "Stat 135", "Stat 135", "Stat 135", "Stat 135",
             "Stat 153", "Stat 153", "Stat 153"),
  start = c(8.5, 8.5, 8.5, 14, 11, 11, 11, 10, 18.5, 18.5, 14),
  end   = c(9.5, 9.5, 9.5, 16, 12, 12, 12, 12, 20, 20, 16)
)

events <- events |>
  mutate(
    day      = as.character(wday(time, label = TRUE)),
    clock    = hour(time) + minute(time) / 60,
    event_id = row_number()
  )

joined <- left_join(events, schedule, by = join_by(day),
                    relationship = "many-to-many")

in_class <- joined |>
  filter(
    clock >= start, clock <= end,
    time >= ymd_hms("2026-08-26 00:00:00", tz = "America/Los_Angeles"),
    time <= ymd_hms("2026-12-12 23:59:59", tz = "America/Los_Angeles")
  )

joined |>
  filter(!is.na(course)) |>
  group_by(course) |>
  summarize(videos_during_class = sum(
    clock >= start & clock <= end &
    time >= ymd_hms("2026-08-26 00:00:00", tz = "America/Los_Angeles") &
    time <= ymd_hms("2026-12-12 23:59:59", tz = "America/Los_Angeles")
  )) |>
  gt()

E. Your Question

How has PinkPantheress’s share of my YouTube watching changed over time?

Answer: She jumped from about 2% of my videos in early 2025 to a peak of about 12% in June 2025, around when Fancy That came out, then settled to a steady 3–6%, with smaller spikes in May and August 2026.

Limitation: this only counts titles that name her, so uploads like “Tonight” with no artist are missed, and Feb 2025 and Oct 2026 are partial months.

E. Your Question: Code

pp_monthly <- events |>
  mutate(
    month = make_datetime(year = year(time), month = month(time), day = 1),
    is_pp = str_detect(song, "pinkpantheress")

F. What Do They Know?

What YouTube could guess about me:

  • When I sleep: I almost never watch between 2 and 7 am
  • My routine: busiest on weekend afternoons and weekday evenings
  • What I like: PinkPantheress most of all, plus Slayyyter, Charli xcx, and EDEN
  • My habits: I replay songs dozens of times and binge new albums

Easiest guess to get wrong: whether I’m paying attention. YouTube only knows a video started. Most of mine are songs playing in the background, so “57 videos during Stat 153” doesn’t mean I was ignoring class.

Would I ask them to delete anything? Yes, my comments and live-chat messages. I wrote those for one moment, not to be saved forever on my account.
